Sub-accounts are shown indented under their parent. Transactions can only be posted to accounts that don't have sub-accounts of their own — parent accounts exist purely to show a rolled-up total on reports and in the Account Ledger search. Accounts marked CASH are the ones counted in dashboard cash totals and the Statement of Cash Flows — this is set explicitly per account, not guessed from the account's name, so an account like "GCash Loan Payable" is never mistaken for an actual cash account just because its name contains the letters "cash".
